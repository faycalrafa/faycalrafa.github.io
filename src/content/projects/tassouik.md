# Tassouik: All-in-One E-Commerce Operations & Backoffice ERP

**Tassouik** is a high-velocity, comprehensive e-commerce backoffice ERP platform engineered for **BFLN Technologies**. Built as an all-in-one operational nerve center, it unified every stage of e-commerce commerce execution — from marketing ad attribution and call center tele-verification to dispatching delivery fleets, warehouse stock management, and financial accounting.

---

## 1. System Vision & The All-in-One Architecture

At **BFLN Technologies**, managing rapid high-volume e-commerce campaigns across multiple product lines required a zero-friction operations platform capable of orchestrating hundreds of orders daily with rapid response times. 

Instead of juggling fragmented third-party tools, **Tassouik** was built with pure **PHP**, responsive **Bootstrap**, and reactive **jQuery** to deliver a blazingly fast, lightweight monolithic architecture with immediate server-side state consistency.

---

## 2. Core Functional Subsystems

### 2.1 Call Center Management Module
- **Real-Time Operator Queues**: Incoming orders automatically route to available telephone confirmation agents based on current workload and language preferences.
- **Call Scripting & History**: Embedded verification prompts, call outcome tracking (*Confirmed*, *Postponed*, *Cancelled*, *Unreachable*), and scheduled automated follow-up reminders.
- **Telephony Analytics**: Per-agent confirmation rates, average handling time (AHT), and automated fraud/duplicate detection algorithms.

### 2.2 Delivery Agents Management & Dispatching
- **Agent Assignment Engine**: Grouped bulk order dispatching by geographic delivery zones and wilayas across Algeria.
- **Live Package Tracking**: Delivery state tracking (*Assigned*, *Picked Up*, *In Transit*, *Attempted*, *Delivered*, *Returned to Warehouse*).
- **Cash-on-Delivery (COD) Reconciliation**: Automated reconciliation between drivers' cash collections, delivery slips, and central treasury accounts.

### 2.3 Financial Accounting, Statistics & Analysis
- **Countability & Performance Metrics**: Real-time revenue summaries, product gross margins, courier return fees, and daily net profitability calculations.
- **Visual Analytics**: Interactive charting and cohort statistics displaying delivery success rates, product turnover velocities, and regional conversion rates.
- **Treasury Audit Trails**: Strict double-entry accounting records preventing reconciliation discrepancies and cash leakage.

### 2.4 Meta Ads Manager Integration
- **Direct API Synchronization**: Synchronized with Facebook & Instagram Ads APIs to correlate ad campaign spend, ad sets, and creatives directly with confirmed purchases.
- **ROAS & Cost-Per-Acquisition (CPA)**: Automated calculation of actual confirmed Return On Ad Spend, factoring in delivery failure and return rates rather than just top-of-funnel clicks.

### 2.5 Multi-Warehouse Logistics
- **Central & Regional Stock Control**: Live stock depletion upon order fulfillment with barcode-ready batch management.
- **Replenishment Forecasting**: Automated alerts for low-inventory thresholds and stock reallocation between main hubs and regional distribution centers.

---

## 3. Technology Stack

- **Server-Side Engine**: Pure PHP 7/8 with robust MVC architecture and high-efficiency PDO database abstractions.
- **Frontend & UI**: Bootstrap 4/5, HTML5, CSS3, and dynamic jQuery client-side modules for instant table filtering and modal workflows.
- **Database**: Relational MySQL with normalized schemas, composite indexing, and transactional locking for concurrency protection.
- **External Integrations**: Meta Graph / Marketing API, SMS Gateway Webhooks, and CSV/Excel batch export pipelines.
- **Hosting**: Dedicated Linux server environment with optimized Nginx web server and automated Bash backup routines.
