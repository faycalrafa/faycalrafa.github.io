# PIASSA DZ: Automotive Parts B2B Marketplace & Supplier CRM

**PIASSA DZ** is an enterprise B2B automotive digital marketplace and supplier Customer Relationship Management (CRM) ecosystem operating across Algeria. During his tenure as **Full-stack Developer** at PIASSA DZ (10/2022 - 12/2023), Faycal Rafa contributed across the entire stack, engineering customer-facing mobile applications, architecting the supplier CRM back-office, and building real-time synchronization pipelines.

---

## 1. Domain Context: Solving Automotive Supply Fragmentation

The automotive aftermarket in emerging markets is traditionally plagued by inventory fragmentation, inaccurate cross-reference part numbers, and slow phone-based quoting. PIASSA DZ digitized this ecosystem by connecting regional parts distributors, automotive repair centers, and fleet managers onto a unified online transactional platform.

---

## 2. Technical Contributions & Architecture

### 2.1 Vehicle VIN Compatibility Search Engine
- Built an extensive relational database indexing millions of Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) parts, aftermarket alternatives, engine serial codes, and vehicle chassis numbers (VINs).
- Designed search algorithms enabling repair technicians to find exact fitment parts in seconds, drastically reducing returns caused by wrong-part ordering.

### 2.2 Supplier CRM & High-Throughput Inventory Sync
- Architected a specialized CRM suite tailored to auto parts wholesalers to manage quotations, credit terms, and order fulfillment.
- Built high-throughput REST APIs and webhooks that synchronize the central marketplace with distributed physical warehouse inventory databases, ensuring real-time stock availability and eliminating phantom listings.

### 2.3 Flutter Mobile Engineering
- Developed cross-platform mobile applications in Flutter/Dart for parts distributors and mechanics on the go.
- Integrated offline-first caching for catalog browsing in low-connectivity repair bays, paired with push notifications for instant order dispatch and delivery tracking.
